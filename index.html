Structural Design and Mechanical Simulation of Vertical Roll-Bending Equipment for Large-Diameter Thin-Walled Rings

BEng Honours Project (individual) · Sep 2025 – Apr 2026

  • Target parts: 12 m diameter rings in 304 stainless steel, 4–10 mm thick and 800–2200 mm wide.
  • Parametric SolidWorks assembly of the uncoiling, roll-bending and cutting units.
  • Strength and stiffness checks: upper-roll (D = 500 mm) deflection 0.24 mm/m; column buckling safety factor 13.7; weld fatigue utilisation 0.70.
  • ANSYS static analysis for 4/6/8/10 mm strip: peak stresses 253.6 / 298.9 / 372.4 / 445.8 MPa, all below 520 MPa.
  • Explicit transient simulation of the forming process predicted a ring diameter of about 10.84 m (within 10% of target).

大直径薄壁圆环立式卷板成形设备结构设计与力学仿真

本科毕业设计（个人项目）· 2025.09 – 2026.04

  • 成形对象：直径 12 m、厚 4–10 mm、宽 800–2200 mm 的 304 不锈钢圆环。
  • 用 SolidWorks 完成开卷、卷板、切割等单元的参数化装配设计。
  • 强度与刚度校核：上辊（D = 500 mm）挠度 0.24 mm/m；立柱屈曲安全系数 13.7；焊缝疲劳利用率 0.70。
  • ANSYS 静力分析：4/6/8/10 mm 板厚下峰值应力分别为 253.6 / 298.9 / 372.4 / 445.8 MPa，均低于 520 MPa。
  • 显式瞬态仿真预测成形直径约 10.84 m，与目标偏差小于 10%。

Mechanical Clock Design Project

Manufacturing Project (group) · Dundee International Institute

  • Calculated gear tooth counts so the second, minute and hour hands keep exact 1/60 and 1/12 ratios, with as few shafts as possible.
  • Built and checked the assembly in SolidWorks, exported part profiles to AutoCAD (DXF) and laser-cut them from acrylic.

Not as Sweet by Another Name: An Empirical Study of Format Robustness in LLM Document Workflows

Xiaoyu Zhang, Xianyun Cheng, Tianlin Li, Yuwei Zheng, Yue Yang, Yang Liu · ASE 2026 (41st IEEE/ACM Int. Conf. on Automated Software Engineering), Munich

  • Question: does changing only the file format of the same document change an LLM's decision in medical, financial and other high-stakes tasks?
  • 3 metamorphic relations across 4 LLM workflows × 4 real tasks × 4 document formats: 48,000 executions.
  • Format changes cut accuracy by up to 53.63% and flipped decisions in over 41% of cases.
  • My part: the lightweight test wrapper and automated experiment pipeline, Jensen–Shannon divergence analysis, and the Vote Aggregation and Format Routing mitigations (up to 44.21% fewer violations).
  • A patent application for the method is in preparation.

Not as Sweet by Another Name: An Empirical Study of Format Robustness in LLM Document Workflows

Xiaoyu Zhang, Xianyun Cheng, Tianlin Li, Yuwei Zheng, Yue Yang, Yang Liu · ASE 2026（CCF-A），慕尼黑

  • 研究问题：在医疗、金融等高风险决策场景中，同一份文档只换文件格式，大模型的判断会不会变？
  • 设计 3 条蜕变关系，覆盖 4 个 LLM 工作流 × 4 类真实任务 × 4 种文档格式，共 48,000 次执行。
  • 结果：仅改变格式，准确率最高下降 53.63%，超过 41% 的样本决策发生漂移。
  • 本人负责：轻量级测试包装器与自动化实验管线、Jensen–Shannon divergence 偏移量化，以及投票聚合、格式路由两种缓解策略（违反率最高降低 44.21%）。
  • 相关方法正在申请发明专利。
